Help

ClimatStress is a sentinel-type aid: it alerts and helps you think about heat, nothing more. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. It does not replace medical advice, the laws and regulations in force, the recommendations of your employer's HSE (Health, Safety, Environment) department or of the occupational health service, nor your own judgement. If you feel unwell, stop work and call the emergency services, whatever the app shows.

Getting started

1. Allow Apple Health access to load your age and resting heart rate. 2. In the Conditions tab, tap 'Get data from Apple Weather' or enter the values yourself. 3. Press Start work on the iPhone (Live tab) or Start on the Apple Watch. If the Watch does not react, open ClimatStress on it. 4. In the Report tab, type the place name; the coordinates are recorded at the start. 5. Follow the alerts.

Heat Index and WBGT

Heat Index uses the NWS Rothfusz regression from air temperature and relative humidity. WBGT follows ISO 7243: outdoors in the sun 0.7·Tnw + 0.2·Tg + 0.1·Ta; indoors 0.7·Tnw + 0.3·Tg. You can also type a value read on an instrument. At night only Heat Index is used.

WBGT: measured, estimated, direct

Direct: type the WBGT read on your instrument. Measured: enter natural wet-bulb (Tnw), globe (Tg) and air (Ta) temperatures; outdoors in the sun WBGT = 0.7 Tnw + 0.2 Tg + 0.1 Ta, indoors 0.7 Tnw + 0.3 Tg (ISO 7243). Estimated: the Liljegren et al. (2008) model (J Occup Environ Hyg 5(10):645–655) computes Tnw and Tg from air temperature, humidity, wind, solar radiation, pressure, time and position; it is a model estimate and can differ from an instrument. Apple Weather wind is assumed measured at 10 m. WBGT is unavailable at night.

Flags and work/rest cycles

The flags are HEAT INDEX flags (°C): below 27 no restriction; 27–31 green; 31.1–38 yellow; 38.1–49 orange; 49.1–53 red; 53.1–59.9 black; 60 and above purple (work forbidden). Work/rest minutes and water reminders come from the flag rules (Settings → Flag rules; defaults 50/10, 40/10, 30/10, 20/10, 20/10). They apply ONLY in Heat Index mode. In WBGT mode the cycle is your approved SITE protocol; the flag shown is an indicative ACGIH 2006 category of the WBGT (moderate work: 25.0, 28.2, 29.0, 30.1, 31.3 °C; light work: 28.1, 30.8, 31.2, 31.8, 32.3 °C) and does not set the minutes. The core-temperature estimate can shorten, never lengthen, these durations.

Purple flag: work forbidden

Purple flag: Heat Index at or above 60 °C. Work is forbidden. A break under this flag is mandatory because of the site conditions, not because of your heart rate. When the flag drops, the break can end through the normal resume check, but the rest is never shorter than 10 minutes. The 10-minute value is the default minimum rest; it is a design choice of ClimatStress.

Breaks and resuming

A haptic and sound alert warns you 5 minutes before the limit. At the limit, tap I'm on break. If you forget, a break is detected only when heart rate has fallen by at least 7 bpm AND you have been still for 2 minutes (both values editable in Settings). A heart-rate rise of 15 bpm (editable) while moving forces a mandatory break, even below 27 °C. After 10 minutes of rest, if your heart rate is still above the resume target you are asked to stay longer. A green screen authorises resuming when HR is at or below the target (resting HR × 1.5 unless you set your own). Movement threshold: 0.05 g, a developer starting value to calibrate (Settings).

Apple Watch

The watch records heart rate through a workout session so alerts keep working with the screen off. Keep the watch snug on the wrist. Connect the iPhone for conditions and settings.

Site chat

The Messages tab is a simple text chat for the people of one site (one group, up to 100 participants). It uses iCloud (CloudKit): there is no ClimatStress server, but every participant needs an iPhone signed in to iCloud. The first person creates the group and enters their name; everyone can then invite others with the link or the QR code (Invite button). A person who leaves or is removed comes back only with a new invitation. Messages older than 48 hours are hidden and deleted by the app when someone opens it, so a message can stay stored longer if nobody opens the app. Notifications are best effort and can be delayed or missing: never rely on this chat for an emergency. Sharing your position sends your coordinates to the whole group. You can report a message or block a person (press and hold a message); reports are stored in the group and you can also write to climatstress@proton.me.

Core temperature estimate

PHS and JOS-3 now show a live predicted rectal temperature on iPhone and Watch and trigger the configured alerts. JOS-3 uses pelvis core, following its original validation. Inputs must be filled explicitly. The model does not guarantee detection of the real core-temperature threshold. Algorithm subject to U.S. rights (U.S. Patent No. 10,702,165 B2, M. J. Buller). This application cannot be distributed in the United States.

Starting temperature from Apple Health

Starting core temperature: the latest Apple Health body temperature is used only if it is less than 12 hours old and statistically close to your own readings of the previous 90 days (within 2 standard deviations, with a floor of 0.2 °C, and at least 10 readings). Otherwise 37.0 °C is used. A wrist or oral reading can be below 37 °C and is then accepted if it is consistent with your own history. These thresholds are design choices of ClimatStress, not published values.

Free access and subscription

All features are free for 3 days from first launch. Afterwards the Heat Index and WBGT calculators stay free; flags and regime, Apple Watch alerts, core-temperature estimate and reports need a subscription (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual) or a lifetime purchase. Use Restore purchases on a new device. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.

Your data

Everything stays on your devices. Reports are saved in the ClimatStress folder of the Files app. Share them to iCloud Drive yourself if you wish. Settings > Delete all stored data erases them. The start date of your free access is kept in the device Keychain. Purchases are handled by Apple. Exception: the optional team chat (Messages tab) stores your name, your messages and any position you share in your iCloud group, visible to its members; see Site chat.
