Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06.

ClimatStress has no account, no advertising and no analytics. The developer does not receive your data.

Data read from Apple Health (with your permission): date of birth (to compute age), resting heart rate, and live heart rate from your Apple Watch during a work session. A workout is saved to Apple Health to keep the session running in the background.

Data you enter: air temperature, humidity, wind, solar radiation, heat index or WBGT values, and your settings.

Location: if you tap the weather button, your approximate location is sent to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) to obtain local weather. Apple's own privacy terms apply to that request. The position is also recorded on your device, at the start of the first work session of the day and when you tap "Use current location", to print the site coordinates in your report; it is never sent to the developer.

Storage: all data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Reports (CSV, PDF) are saved in the ClimatStress folder of the Files app. ClimatStress does not write health data to iCloud; if you copy a report to iCloud Drive yourself, that copy is governed by your iCloud settings.

Sharing: nothing is shared with third parties. You decide whether to share a report.

Retention and deletion: data is kept until you delete it. Settings > Delete all stored data erases reports stored by the app; uninstalling the app removes its data. You can revoke Health and Location access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases: subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple (StoreKit). The developer does not receive your payment details or Apple Account identity.

Free access: the date of first launch is stored in the device Keychain on your device to compute the 3-day free period. It is not sent anywhere.

Team chat (optional, version 2.0.0): if you use it, your chosen display name, your messages and, only if you tap the position button, your coordinates are stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit) in a group shared by the people of your site, and are visible to every member of that group. The developer runs no server and cannot read them. Messages are hidden and deleted after 48 hours by the app (a message can stay stored longer if nobody opens the app). Your iCloud account is used by Apple to identify you; the app only keeps a pseudonymous identifier derived from it. You can report a message, block a person, leave the group or, if you own it, delete it. Without iCloud, or if you never open the Messages tab, nothing is sent.

Children: ClimatStress is intended for working adults.
