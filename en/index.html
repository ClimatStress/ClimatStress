ClimatStress

Heat-stress decision support for construction sites

What it does

  • Heat Index or WBGT with the equations of the literature (NWS Rothfusz; ISO 7243).
  • Work/rest cycles by flag (green to black), as maxima.
  • Apple Watch alerts: haptic and sound 5 minutes before the break, green light to resume.
  • Heart-rate based core-temperature estimate (model, not a measurement).
  • Daily HSE report in CSV and PDF. English, French, Spanish, Portuguese.
  • WBGT measured, direct or estimated with the Liljegren et al. (2008) model; flags from ACGIH 2006 values for WBGT. Editable thresholds. All features free for 3 days, then the Heat Index and WBGT calculators stay free; subscriptions (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual) or a lifetime purchase unlock the rest.
  • Team chat (optional): one text group per site through iCloud, 48 hours of history, invitation by link or QR code. Not for emergencies.

Requires iPhone with iOS 18 or later and Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later.

ClimatStress is a sentinel-type aid: it alerts and helps you think about heat, nothing more. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. It does not replace medical advice, the laws and regulations in force, the recommendations of your employer's HSE (Health, Safety, Environment) department or of the occupational health service, nor your own judgement. If you feel unwell, stop work and call the emergency services, whatever the app shows.
